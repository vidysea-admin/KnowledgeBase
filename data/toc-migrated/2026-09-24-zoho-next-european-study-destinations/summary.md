## Hungary: IBS Budapest (Anjum)
- **Growth:** she has promoted Hungary for 12 years. Admissions went from 1 to 10 a year to about 200 to 250 Indian students a year. Selling points: affordable fees, a safe country, simple visa process. [16:02]
- **Degrees:** every programme (bachelor's, master's, PhD) awards the IBS degree plus a University of Buckingham degree, with no need to travel to the UK. [16:09]
  - Dual-degree bachelor's programmes: Business Administration, Business Economics, Psychology, and Marketing & Digital Media, with Dublin Business School (Ireland), De Montfort University (UK or UAE campus) and BHMS (Switzerland).
  - Dual-degree study splits: 2 years in Budapest plus 2 years at the partner (Dublin), or 2 years plus the final year (De Montfort).
- **Scholarships:** [16:12]
  - A bachelor's applicant with IELTS 6.5 (no band below 6) gets 25% off the first semester.
  - After that, up to 100% per semester, based on the previous semester's grades.
  - Students without IELTS can join on an MOI (medium-of-instruction) letter.
  - Past internships don't count towards scholarships. [16:52]
- **Jobs:** [16:19 to 16:29]
  - IBS is the only university inside a business park with about 70 multinationals, next door to Microsoft and SAP.
  - The IBS Talent Hub job portal stays open to graduates for life.
  - Official hiring partners: Microsoft, Cognizant, Genpact, Emirates, Tata, Tesco, Accor, Citibank. Citibank came to hire about 700 people.
  - Part-time: about €800 to €1,000 a month for 20 hours a week.
  - Master's classes start at 5 pm, so master's students can work full time.
  - Starting salaries €1,200 to €1,500 a month; finance, AI and cybersecurity about €2,000 to €2,200.
- **Living costs:** about €500 a month in total. Accommodation €100 on campus or €250 to €300 outside, groceries about €50, and a monthly transport pass (quoted as "3 euros").
- **Post-study work permit:** 9 months from immigration plus 6 more from IBS, 15 months in total. [16:43]
  - Bachelor's students interview at the 5th-semester placement fair; master's students in the 3rd semester, which carries no tuition fee.
  - Job contracts run at least 2 years.
  - Permanent residency after 5 years, counting the study years.
  - If a graduate hasn't found a job: a 6-month extension for about €700, renewable.
- **Admissions:** [16:56]
  - Age limits: bachelor's up to 24, Budapest master's 30 to 32 (33 for exceptional profiles).
- **Proof of funds:** [16:58]
  - A 6-month bank statement in the student's or parents' name, not a third party's.
  - About €18,000 to €20,000 for a master's, €20,000 recommended for a bachelor's.
  - Money must be at least a month old with normal transactions; a single lump-sum deposit gets rejected.
  - Parents' funding needs their tax return (ITR). A loan must already be paid out; a sanction letter alone doesn't count.
- **Austria, IBS Vienna:** one programme, the Master's in International Management. [16:36]
  - No age limit.
  - The university's lawyer files the visa, with zero rejections so far.
  - No bachelor's needed with 3 years of managerial experience after class 12. Realistically that means applicants aged 26 or older.
- **Teaching:** 70% practical, many industry lecturers (for example a Microsoft marketing head), at most 35 students per batch.

## Greece: Hellenic American University (Sagar)
- **Why now:** the image is shifting from tourism to education. [16:05]
  - It's an American degree taught in English, with low living costs.
  - Promotional master's fee: USD 18,900 instead of USD 35,600.
- **Class size:** 12:1 student-to-faculty ratio, and a mentor for the capstone project (the equivalent of India's "black book"). [16:15]
- **Careers:** a career counselling office and a campus career day.
- **Programmes:** [16:30]
  - About 70% of students take the MBA or BSBA.
  - Bachelor's in Informatics covers CS, AI, game development, app development and UX.
  - Also psychology, music and engineering.
  - The master's in psychology has two tracks, clinical psychology and mental health counselling. It's popular with Indian students and open to any bachelor's.
- **Easy admission:** low percentage requirements; MOI, open-school and distance-learning degrees are accepted; backlogs are fine as long as the degree is completed.
- **US transfer:** after 1 year in Athens, business bachelor's and master's students can move to the US campus (NECHE accredited). It's a campus transfer, not a credit transfer, with the same degree. [16:32, 16:48]
- **Exchange:** a student-exchange programme of 4 to 12 weeks for credit; one Indian batch came last year.
- **Student life:** 20 nationalities on campus, plus an international office. [16:41]
- **Intakes:** three a year (fall, spring and summer), with every programme open all year. [16:51]
- **Study gaps:** up to 4 years accepted for a bachelor's and 10 for a master's; short diplomas don't count toward the gap. Every applicant has a one-to-one interview at the Greek embassy. [16:55]
- **Proof of funds:** about USD 24,000 to 25,000 for a master's. For the other level it was quoted as USD 19,000 to 20,000; Sagar said "masters" both times, so this was probably meant as the bachelor's figure. [16:57]
  - Money a month old, with a 3-month bank statement. A loan must already be paid out.
- **Spouses:** no dependant visa while studying; spouses can visit on a visitor visa. [16:53]
  - A family-reunification visa is possible after getting a work permit, but it is quota-based and slots can close.

## For counsellors
- Match each student's profile, budget and background to a destination instead of choosing by popularity. Add Hungary and Greece alongside France, Germany, Ireland and Spain.
- HAU's route of an American degree in Europe followed by a transfer to the US campus is a strong option now that direct US applications from India are down to about 10%. [16:47]
- Both universities will share their slide decks and brochures with partners after the webinar. [16:59]
