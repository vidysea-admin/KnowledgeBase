## Remaining blocker (owner decision; I did not change it)
`packages/index/src/pipeline/notes/sentence-span.ts` `isBoundary` rejects true sentence ends, so the verifier drops quotes that are whole sentences. Replayed offline on the logged claims reply for t006[0:4000]: 12 of 28 claims rejected (7 starts-mid-sentence, 5 ends-mid-sentence), e.g. the quote "So, we do a diagnostic first, always." sits between "of an exam. " and " So, all exams"; and "The Indian aptitude test, I started ..." follows "SAT, GRE, GMAT. ". Causes in `isBoundary`: an ALL-CAPS token before ". " (GMAT., TOC., PTE.) is never a boundary (`ordinary` requires all lowercase); a lowercase token of <=4 letters (exam.) before a capital fails closed when a negation/qualifier marker ("only") appears later in the sentence. After drops, the judge sees one survivor of 29 and correctly says the extraction is incomplete. Relaxing this is a verifier-bar change (ISS-T040GEN-010/-012 history), so it is the Approver's call. Smallest candidate: treat an all-caps token of >=2 letters followed by ". " + capital as a boundary.
