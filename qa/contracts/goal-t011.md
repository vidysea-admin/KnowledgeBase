## Full acceptance criteria

1. **Real production entry and connected auto-join.** An actual CLI/API/scheduler caller reaches
   the real meeting runtime. Connected Calendar/discovery candidates and the authorized rules
   select an eligible meeting, honor opt-out/refusal decisions and record why excluded candidates
   did not join. Show one accepted real candidate progressing into actual capture without a
   manually substituted fixture URL. Retain the per-source priority/scope authorized by D-165.

2. **Policy and consent facts stay truthful.** Operational authority, such as D-165/Umesh's
   deployment authorization, is distinguished from actual organizer/source consent facts.
   A title, default or deployment authorization must not fabricate per-meeting `given: true`,
   an actor or confirmation. Preserve the selected capture mode, actual supplied consent facts,
   approval provenance, notes and alternative-confirmation facts through capture, finalization,
   recovery and ingestion. Evaluate provided-first policy before joining. D-008's unconfirmed
   alternatives warning remains SOFT; this contract introduces no new organizer-consent rule.
   Invalid or unauthorized requests follow the existing recorded policy and fail explicitly.

3. **Actual host join and media.** On the claimed supported host, a real supported meeting/webinar
   is joined using the configured account/profile and produces playable, non-silent media from
   that meeting. Evidence identifies platform, host, runtime invocation and resulting artifact.
   Unsupported transports/platforms report a clear limitation; they cannot return fabricated
   join/capture success. Platform expansion remains separately covered by T-045; native tab
   capture duration/parallelism and lean-size targets remain T-034/T-035 acceptance gates.

4. **Bounded permission-safe controls and isolation.** Preserve the adopted live-capture
   permission-denial, exact allowlist and click limits. Never enable mic/camera or capture
   unrelated desktop/microphone audio as a fallback. Auth-derived tenant/user identity binds
   profile, controls, capture artifacts and receipts. Independent negative checks cover profile
   traversal/collision, foreign tenant/session control or recovery, and credentials/cookies in
   logs, API responses or tracked artifacts. Real claimed isolation requires host evidence.

5. **Live status and privacy reach the runtime.** Actual status reflects joining, recording,
   interrupted/failed and finished states, with actionable reasons. A live private-window control
   reaches the capture/transcript path; overlapping turns are excluded before persisted transcript,
   ingest/index and other knowledge consumers see them. Profile/private-filter primitives or a
   monitor screen alone are insufficient. Prove normal and boundary-overlap exclusions and
   ensure a foreign tenant/session cannot read status or alter privacy controls.

6. **Stop, failure and restart are observable and safe.** Required adopted cleanup/recovery
   behavior is proven on the actual host: controlled stop, join/capture failure and controller
   interruption/recovery preserve valid output, restore only owned resources/mutes and do not
   terminate another run or report incomplete media as success. Gaps and failure reasons remain
   discoverable. A real interruption/recovery receipt supplements focused failure-path checks.

7. **The captured output reaches the immediate downstream stage.** A real authorized recording
   passes the silence/duration checks and the configured transcription stage; resulting source,
   session and timestamped turns are valid and consumed by tenant-scoped ingest/index. Read-back
   proves that citation references resolve to the same tenant/session and reprocessing is scoped
   and idempotent. No silent or failed capture calls transcription or publishes invented turns.
   A cited downstream retrieval/Ask consumption receipt proves the captured knowledge is usable;
   full extraction/search quality remains governed by the separate task contracts.

8. **Independent current-candidate verification.** The maker supplies a ready-for-check manifest,
   current fix cycle, complete changed-path/dependency map and final hashes. The checker reviews
   real caller/consumer and security seams and runs the smallest meaningful independent normal,
   failure and security checks under the CPU lease. Issue-fix checks replay the ledger's recorded
   cases verbatim and report counts by issue ID. Reused historical evidence is explicitly
   attributed and cannot certify changed bytes or replace missing current integration proof.

9. **Required baseline checks and receipts.** Preserve applicable adopted baseline/no-regression
   criteria and the frozen-contract verifier. Existing identical base failures may be disclosed
   only under the adopted contract's exception; a newly introduced failure prevents PASS.
   Every necessary receipt records exact command, timestamp/duration, exit/result, fixture or
   live resource, checked bytes and output/artifact path. Shared-machine limits determine when
   checks can run; they do not waive required acceptance.

10. **Parent verdict is explicit.** Parent PASS requires all criteria above with actual runtime,
    live/host and downstream evidence. Missing credentials, host access, activation approval,
    current-candidate provenance or live evidence produce HOLD with named outstanding gates;
    a reproducible acceptance violation produces FAIL. A narrowly scoped engineering PASS
    must state the remaining parent gates and cannot mark T-011 done. Only the independent
    checker can issue PASS; maker close-out must match the checked slug and cycle.
