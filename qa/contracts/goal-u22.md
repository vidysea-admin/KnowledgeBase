# U2.2 extraction review workflow and parent acceptance

Checker-owned acceptance established 2026-10-14 before implementation. Priority: tier 3 authorized roadmap continuation. Decisions D-108 and D-163 and existing extraction adjudication/draft contracts remain authoritative. This contract does not authorize production/provider calls, source repairs, global gold, threshold invention or whole-task completion from a preparation slice.

## Full U2.2 acceptance (remaining parent gates)

P1. Independently reconcile the real corpus: original 72 claims, all 80 literal citation occurrences, 14 unresolved reference identities and seven separate replacement proposals. Current source and manifest hashes must bind measured bytes; unavailable corpus cases/stages remain acquisition failures or unavailable, never successful zero-row measurements.

P2. Measure raw, parsed, filtered and persisted extraction separately when acquired, retaining fabrication evidence before filtering. Missing stages remain explicitly unavailable. Citation existence/time/attribution and semantic support are distinct; unsupported literal citations neither prove factual falsity nor full-transcript absence.

P3. D-163 requires actual individual Umesh review/approval before a draft becomes human gold. JSON declarations alone do not prove that authorization. Semantic/fact/entity gold, coverage and omissions must be independently reviewed and hash-bound; missing fact targets/entity labels remain unknown, never fabricated or treated as zero errors.

P4. Binding quality thresholds require separate explicit approval and measured acceptance; no implied threshold or release PASS. Weighted rates retain numerators/denominators and label coverage. Required validator, affected real-data stage and immediate downstream consumer evidence must be complete before parent PASS. U2.2 remains in_progress until these gates are satisfied.

## Scoped local individual-review workflow acceptance

S1. Local editor consumes all 72 existing drafts exactly once, retaining all original claims/evidence, full current/historical contexts and separate proposed replacements. Its identities/hash bindings must independently verify against the unchanged source batches/packet. Initial human decisions remain pending and humanGold false; original drafts/sources/runtime stay untouched.

S2. Each item can be independently reviewed with explicit decision and a supported/partial/unsupported/unavailable declaration, reviewer identity/date and rationale. Existing draft values may be shown as AI drafts but never auto-approved. Complete export preserves all 72 entries, including pending entries. No global/bulk approval or gold promotion mechanism is included.

S3. Strict validation refuses malformed documents, missing/duplicate/unknown items, wrong corpus/item/hash identities, invalid decisions/labels/reviewer/date, inconsistent pending/declared states and unsupported approval or threshold fields. Missing/incomplete approvals cannot be silently promoted. Merely naming Umesh in exported data remains a declaration, not proof of actual authorization.

S4. Offline bridge validates export and exposes declaration coverage separately while retaining humanGold false. Its existing measurement consumer runs without imported semantic/fact/entity labels or binding thresholds until actual item-specific human authorization is recorded through a separately accepted path. Partial declarations remain honest; missing raw/parsed/filtered and fact/entity data stay unavailable.

S5. Generation is byte-idempotent and refuses differing existing output rather than overwriting reviewed content. Source batches and existing extraction artifacts remain byte-identical. Independently exercise editor interactions without a browser, validator acceptance/refusal boundaries, affected-stage output and immediate measurement consumer. Record exact candidate cycle/final hashes, command/output and frozen-contract validator. Scoped PASS certifies S1-S5 only and explicitly reports unmet P1-P4.
