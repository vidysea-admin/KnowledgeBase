## Acceptance criteria

1. **Recorded protection, not a supplied assertion.** Completeness exemptions come only from independently validated evidence produced by the approved capture/privacy path that specific portions were intentionally protected in this recording. Evidence must belong to the actual tenant, session and recording generation. Arbitrary `source.privateWindows`, a caller-supplied array, or metadata that merely declares a gap private cannot waive missing speech. Missing privacy evidence retains ordinary strict completeness. Purported privacy evidence that is malformed, stale, foreign or inconsistent fails closed rather than falling back to an exempt state.

2. **Exact temporal meaning.** The protocol validates finite ordered intervals inside independently probed audio duration and defines boundary/overlap normalization and offset behavior. Span-local and absolute recording coordinates must reconcile through single-call selection, chunking and recursive tail recovery. Do not add a blanket time tolerance or gap threshold that exempts unprotected content; approved existing completeness thresholds retain their separate purpose.

3. **Only the actual protected deficit is waived.** Driver completeness, retry/recursive decisions and downstream validation use the same trusted coverage semantics. A mixed gap remains incomplete for its unprotected portion. Ordinary leading, internal and tail deficits still fail under the applicable existing rules. Excessively broad, shifted, duplicated, malformed or forged intervals cannot convert an incomplete transcript into a complete one. A recording with no usable public speech cannot be promoted merely because all time is marked protected.

4. **Privacy persists through transcription and knowledge admission.** Approved upstream redaction/capture protection remains effective on both single and chunked paths. Retries or recursive recovery cannot refill protected speech, and generated assertions within intentionally excluded regions cannot become accepted knowledge. The packet must show how actual protected content is handled at provider and downstream boundaries according to the approved protocol; metadata alone is not evidence of redaction. Do not silently relax privacy to recover coverage.

5. **Bound and honest artifacts.** Persist verified intentional exclusions, provenance and the actual coverage disposition with the recording/transcript/QA generation. Changing source/privacy evidence invalidates prior validation even if final canonical turns are identical. Existing finite exact bounds, accepted-span hallucination signals, audit/count conservation, speech and QA digest/presence validation, and pending/atomic publication stay enforced. Reports distinguish protected absence from ordinary missing content and disclose partial coverage honestly. Intentional exclusions are not an unrestricted `--accept-partial` bypass and do not resolve ISS-REVALIDATE-002.

6. **Real immediate callers and consumer.** The accepted helper must be wired through the actual driver and actual downstream validator/admission path, not only through a pure helper or screen. Shared driver/loader/watch composition is coordinated by its leased owners. A clean trusted-exclusion sample must be consumed by the immediate downstream stage without handwritten passed validation or unvalidated source overrides; inconsistent/foreign/stale privacy evidence and mixed unprotected deficits must refuse.

7. **Independent evidence and immutable candidate.** A distinct READY manifest/packet identifies this unit's exact scope, fix cycle, changed/new owner paths, complete final source/dependency/contract pins and commands/results. Focused evidence and an independent hostile probe run only under a fresh sole CPU-broker slot. Cover trusted valid exclusions, absent evidence, forged/stale/malformed/foreign evidence, boundary/offset cases, mixed deficits, selected versus abandoned retries, same-output/new-privacy evidence and publication failure. Issue-fix claims replay their recorded ledger corpus and name omissions. Reuse unchanged earlier evidence with exact attribution; earlier scoped PASS cannot certify changed source. Recheck final pins before verdict. Required shared frozen verifier evidence remains separate and explicitly vacuous if the registry is empty.
