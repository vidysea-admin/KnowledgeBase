## Scoped acceptance

1. **Bounded genuine navigation.** The production Ask selection path navigates authorized source trees using source-derived routing summaries plus keyword/graph signals. The entire catalogue is never sent to a provider. Each frontier, completion, aggregate input/output, call count, traversal depth and candidate count has a measured enforced bound. A catalogue larger than the former 64 KiB admission ceiling remains navigable or refuses explicitly; raising an unlimited ceiling is not acceptance. Duplicate/cyclic identifiers, hostile metadata, excessive depth, missing branches and provider selections outside the offered frontier refuse safely.
2. **Exact evidence only.** Routing summaries and graph descriptions cannot serve as answer evidence. Selected authorized leaves hydrate current exact passages with source identity, speaker/attribution where applicable, and source-native locators. Transcript evidence retains verified quote offsets, speaker and ordered timestamps; documents use real pages/sections and messages use real thread/message locators without invented audio timestamps. Forged IDs, foreign joins, invalid offsets/hashes or stale revisions cannot become citations.
3. **No vectors.** Active mounted Ask/search, correction-triggered retrieval, restart and retry paths perform zero embedding-provider calls, vector database queries/writes or vector-ranking operations. Inject throwing spies at these boundaries; absent legacy vectors cannot degrade an otherwise valid vectorless result. Dormant optional adapters may remain.
4. **Language and empty evidence.** English, Hindi/Devanagari and Hinglish fixtures retrieve their actual relevant source passage. Unicode token handling preserves meaningful words. Empty queries, no overlap, no valid source, provider failure and partial evidence produce truthful empty/refusal/degraded results; no answer is manufactured from route metadata.
5. **Security and lifecycle.** Verified tenant and approval/quarantine eligibility constrain reads before tree/graph selection and passage hydration. Foreign and pending/rejected source labels/text are not sent to the provider. Concurrent edit, revocation, deletion and tenant/source collisions cannot retain stale trusted evidence or cache hits. Source instructions remain untrusted data and cannot override retrieval/citation controls.
6. **Compatibility and downstream.** Existing /ask and /search contracts remain compatible; capabilities/UI describe tree/keyword retrieval honestly. Exercise affected selection with representative real source data and the immediate hydration/answer consumer, including source links. Shared production wiring is separately checked at integration; a module-only PASS cannot certify mounted runtime.
