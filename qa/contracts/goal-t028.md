## Remaining criteria

1. **C1 Verified identity and permission boundary.** Mounted account routes derive tenant and actor/key identity from server-side API-key verification and use the T-060 role authority. Body/header/query tenant, actor or role values cannot substitute for verified identity. Missing, malformed, revoked, unbound and insufficiently privileged callers fail closed. Exercise administrative lifecycle/grant permissions and lower-role refusal; no caller may elevate its own authority through an account payload or use another tenant's account/key identifiers. No invented password/SSO credential flow is required or credited.
2. **C2 Durable counsellor lifecycle.** Authenticated create, list/read, update and deactivate operations actually persist tenant-scoped counsellor state, remain discoverable through a reconstructed store/runtime, validate hostile/malformed input and define duplicate/not-found behavior. Deactivation has an enforced effect on the associated active authority/consumer, rather than merely changing an unused flag. All read/write predicates and identifiers remain tenant-confined; foreign IDs cannot reveal or mutate another tenant's account.
3. **C3 Grants use the real authority.** Grant and revoke operations use documented T-060 role/permission boundaries and durable account/key associations. Privilege updates take effect in the immediate verified consumer, including after store reconstruction; inactive/deactivated counsellors cannot continue through the covered authority path. Caller-supplied grants/scopes cannot bypass the administrative route. Missing identity/role/durable dependencies fail honestly, without permissive defaults or a test-only identity mapping.
4. **C4 Durable audit and atomic failure boundary.** Account lifecycle and grant mutations produce immutable tenant-scoped audit evidence with verified actor, operation, target, outcome and timestamp, without raw credentials or unnecessary personal data. Audit failure prevents unaudited mutation success; intermediate persistence failures have a defined recoverable/refused state. Exercise write/audit failure and relevant concurrent/replay boundaries. Security findings remain uncapped.
5. **C5 Immediate user/runtime consumption.** Review the mounted API composition, durable store and actual accounts page/consumer. A bounded sample demonstrates verified authentication -> authorized create/update/grant -> list/read -> deactivate -> covered consumer refusal -> audit read, including another tenant's refusal cases and reconstruction. The page exposes meaningful lifecycle/grant success and failure states using real account endpoints. Primitive-only or test-only composition, static checks and unrelated Gmail/calendar evidence cannot certify this runtime.
6. **C6 Independent handshake and delivery evidence.** Require a matching ready-for-check manifest, fix cycle, packet, changed/caller/consumer map and final byte pins. Independently run a meaningful focused boundary/consumer probe under the root serial CPU lease, retain applicable `contracts/verify_contracts.py` evidence and identify reused maker evidence precisely. Changed candidate bytes/cycle require resubmission. A demonstrated violation is FAIL; missing required runtime evidence is HOLD.
7. **C7 Real account/deployment scope honesty.** Real credentials/accounts, production writes, promotion and deployment proofs retain their separate existing gates. Fixture/local evidence must be identified as such. A scoped fixture/runtime PASS cannot alone mark whole-task live delivery done; the verdict names any remaining account/deployment gates and only certifies its checked scope.
