   source associations and raw WhatsApp nodes/extras. A removed unsafe child or descendant

   Whole-source parent summaries are withheld when their provenance cannot distinguish reviewed
   WhatsApp facts. Detached WhatsApp nodes contain only current independently approved fact
   content/ids/turn references, never siblings, raw titles or attachment context. Input is not mutated.
6. **Independent verification and integration accounting.** Same-cycle final SHA256s, complete

   schema/structure/integration checks remain required. Actual Brain/Ask tree/vector/lexical,
