## Acceptance criteria

1. **Actual configured production path.** A documented operator command or application flow consumes an authentic recorded-session transcript through the actual configured extraction and independent note-judge providers, persists canonical grounded output, and exposes it through the mounted authenticated grounded-content route and actual session-detail consumer. Respect D-162's default-off grounded-note flag: an explicitly selected flag may enable the acceptance run; this contract does not authorize changing its default. Provider fakes, fixture-only persistence, a read-only inspector, or a standalone component do not establish this criterion.

2. **Complete output scope.** The resulting session output supports summary, key facts including numbers, Q&A, and action items. Use authentic sample content that contains supported examples of these categories, or explicitly identify and independently inspect additional authentic samples for missing categories. Do not infer success merely from one nonempty insight. A category genuinely absent from a source may be empty with an explicit source-supported absence; invented padding is forbidden. Numbers preserve their units, dates, bounds and qualifications. No new schema category is required when canonical insights/claims carry facts and numbers. Decisions, when produced, retain cited adoption or commitment rather than treating proposals as decisions.

3. **Per-item provenance.** Every published summary item, fact/number, action, question and non-null answer has its own nonempty evidence. Each evidence reference resolves to the same tenant and source session, an existing turn, the actual speaker identity/label, finite ordered nonnegative turn timestamps, and an exact contiguous quote from that turn. Generated grounded item text follows its applicable extractive contract; preserved human-reviewed claim text is not required to become a verbatim transcript slice when its canonical schema allows a paraphrase. Displayed timestamp links target the matching source turn. A generic all-turn evidence list does not substitute for item-level citations. OCR and visual observations preserve their distinct origin and unverified status.

4. **Grounding and semantic restraint.** The strict quote/span gate and configured independent judge both apply to summary items, claims and answers, including answers found in later windows. Lexical safety rules also apply when the judge is enabled (D-151). Retractions, negation, conditions, ownership and deadlines cannot be dropped to change meaning. Suggestions do not become assigned actions; absent owners or dates are not inferred. Generated output remains visibly unverified rather than acquiring human verification from a successful quote match or judge verdict. Reuse exact attributed existing-unit evidence for established boundaries, with candidate compatibility checked; an inspector's quote lookup alone is not proof of semantic correctness.

5. **Q&A correctness.** Question and answer evidence remain separately available. Unanswered or rejected answers have `answer: null`, `status: unanswered`, and empty answer evidence. Fabricated or unsupported questions are refused. A late answer is kept only when it is relevant and independently judged; unrelated nearby text cannot answer a question merely because it has a valid quote.

6. **Coverage and truthful failure.** Windowed extraction accounts for all submitted source spans, with long turns and late questions handled without silently omitting required input. Invalid coverage acknowledgements, unsupported content, provider/judge failure and indexing degradation produce explicit drops, failures or gaps. A failed or degraded strict run must not be presented as complete/ready. Verify at least one relevant failure boundary and the long-window/late-answer boundary, using existing pinned standing evidence where it actually covers the final path. Source absence and unavailable evidence stay distinguishable from successful processing with zero relevant content.

7. **Safe canonical persistence and rerun.** Canonical session pages and claims are tenant-scoped and schema-valid; references agree with the stored source transcript. A repeat run does not erase human-reviewed claims, cross tenant boundaries or create unexplained replacement/duplication. Failure cannot delete existing useful knowledge before successful replacement. Preserve existing-unit guarantees and demonstrate actual work-database readback of the acceptance sample; fixture writes are insufficient for full task acceptance. A read-only inspector cannot supply missing generation/persistence evidence.

8. **Real downstream consumption.** The production authenticated route returns the persisted sample's grounded categories with resolved citations and rejects foreign-tenant/unknown sessions. The actual mounted session-detail page consumes that response, displays separate question/answer evidence and unverified labels, and its evidence link reaches the real matching transcript turn. Capture attributed route/readback and application-consumer proof. Source-only mount review or mocked DOM may establish scoped integration, but must be labelled as such and cannot silently replace required real consumer proof. If browser execution is unavailable under the current CPU/access lease, this criterion remains unverified.

9. **Evidence and scope honesty.** A maker manifest and packet identify the fix cycle, exact commands, exit codes/results, authentic source/session identity, hashes of relevant source and output, provider/model identity and persistence target without secrets. Checker verifies candidate pins and independent behavior. The frozen-contract validator must pass, with empty-registry vacuity disclosed. Existing unit PASSes establish only their written scope; a complete-task PASS requires all criteria above. No production deployment, unattended release, capture/transcription quality completion, public launch or T-051 completion follows from T-040 acceptance.
