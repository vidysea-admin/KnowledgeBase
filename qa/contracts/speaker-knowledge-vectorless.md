# Acceptance contract — speaker-knowledge-vectorless

Checker-owned, 2026-10-15. Authority: Umesh's explicit speaker/session brain and approved Knowledge Brain plan; D-173 as narrowly restored by D-174. Priority tier 3, U2.4/T-055/T-056. Historical graph/profile/privacy contracts remain binding.

## Scoped acceptance

1. **Confirmed identity only.** Individual speaker views belong to the shared tenant graph. Stable confirmed identity/aliases connect actual sessions and attributed statements. Unconfirmed suggestions, unknown speaker tokens and fuzzy similarity cannot merge people or appear as confirmed identities. Two tenants sharing names/IDs remain isolated.
2. **Read authority precedes projection.** Source visibility, publication status and tenant checks apply before profile/graph hydration or provider dispatch. Raw helper inputs cannot widen the already-authorized product profile. Pending/rejected messages, hidden sessions, foreign joins and stale published generations contribute no labels, counts, passages or relationships.
3. **Source-backed statements.** Every substantive statement/relationship resolves to exact current source evidence. Speaker ownership is checked against the attributed source, not merely the relation subject. Supporting quotes, real turn/page/message membership, source revision and applicable times are validated. Routing summaries, co-occurrence, similar text and LLM assertions are not factual evidence. Derived associations are explicitly inferred; agreement/disagreement/revision is never manufactured from co-occurrence.
4. **Freshness and honest gaps.** Corrections, revoked approval, deletions and changed attribution remove stale trusted statements/relations and invalidate cached profiles. Contradictory or changed advice preserves separate source/context/date rather than collapsing into a single truth. Missing evidence is excluded with truthful degradation rather than fabricated details.
5. **Bounded traversal and consumer.** Per-speaker statements, sessions, neighbours, expansion depth and prompt bytes are bounded deterministically. Unicode/Hinglish speaker/topic inputs remain usable. Product profile/graph consumer exposes drill-down to the actual source evidence; a raw helper alone cannot certify mounted UI/API integration.

## Required probes and verdict limits

Independent fixtures include colliding tenant speaker IDs, confirmed/unconfirmed aliases, known speaker with no source, own subject but foreign speaker quote, pending source, changed transcript hash, forged evidence, missing exact quote, duplicate edges, bounded large inventory and source revocation. Reuse existing authorized profile-store boundary only with matching checked bytes and consumer proof. All local execution goes through sole broker. Same-cycle manifest/final hashes, affected stage, immediate downstream consumer and frozen verifier are required. A scoped projection PASS leaves full identity precision, human confirmation, mounted browser, live corpus and full parent acceptance open.
