# Acceptance contract — vectorless-publication

Checker-owned, 2026-10-15. Authority: Umesh's approved Knowledge Brain plan and D-173 as narrowly restored by D-174. Priority tier 3, T-008/T-043/T-054. Historical vector criteria remain recorded; this contract governs active vectorless publication only.

## Scoped acceptance

1. **Readiness without embeddings.** Ordinary and strict webinar publication require valid current source/turn evidence, grounded derived knowledge and current tree/lexical visibility. Successful publication never requires embeddings or legacy vectors and never creates a vector-pending gap when intentionally vectorless. Missing/invalid transcript, tree, grounded evidence or source authority remains a visible failure; removing an embedding gate cannot weaken those checks.
2. **Zero vector operations.** Instrument actual indexing, reindex, transcript correction, restart and retry callsites with embedding/vector boundaries that throw if touched. Passing an embed function accidentally cannot activate it in vectorless mode. Active API/worker/script composition must select vectorless, including strict webinar and correction paths. Explicit dormant legacy mode is not active-product acceptance.
3. **Atomic current generation.** Source revisions bind page, claims, lexical material, tree entries and publication receipts. A failed/stale concurrent attempt cannot replace the last accepted complete generation or certify mixed artifacts. Retries and unchanged reindex are idempotent; interruption, duplicate delivery and stale ownership cannot duplicate corpus or overwrite a newer accepted result.
4. **Evidence and authority.** Every indexed item has tenant and stable source identity plus exact valid evidence. Source-native document/message locators remain honest. Pending/rejected/quarantined or foreign material cannot publish. Source update, review revocation/deletion and correction invalidate prior derived evidence and caches; retained stale rows are unreadable as current knowledge.
5. **Downstream proof.** After publication, current source is discoverable through tree/keyword retrieval and exact citations resolve in Ask/search. A mere success status or fake receipt is insufficient. Failure/retry must retain honest readiness diagnostics; provider/database failure cannot claim all-source indexing success.

## Evidence and verdict limits

Same-cycle final hashes, command/results/timing and affected/downstream sample proof are required through the sole broker. Independent probes cover foreign joins, failed strict preflight, optional throwing embedder, interrupted generation, unchanged replay and source revocation. Frozen validator remains required and its empty registry disclosed. Scoped source PASS does not establish real Mongo persistence/restart, full source integrations, real capture/provider acceptance, 92-question quality or full parent closure without those additional receipts.
