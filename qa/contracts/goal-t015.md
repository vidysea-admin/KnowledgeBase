## Ground truth and scope

Sources: TASKS.md T-015; `.goal/goal.json` T-015 (depends on T-002, marked done); `.goal/catalogue.json` E7; docs/goal.md [02:35–02:46]; ARCHITECTURE.md H3/H5/H6; D-170; G10/R3 retained/deferred policy in TASKS.md and goal.json; AGENTS.md; skills/checker/SKILL.md.

The original goal is to train an own model using attributed knowledge while keeping that information out of worldwide models. T-015 requires explicit approval before **any data export**. D-170 authorizes unblocked implementation, not approval of a particular corpus, training run, external destination or promotion. Ingestion/capture consent, a source's public availability, an enabled feature flag and a user-provided `approved: true` field do not establish training/export authorization. G10 remains retained/deferred; it need not activate for R3, and deferral cannot be counted as T-015 completion.

An independently checked preparation unit may PASS its complete named scope without a real export or training run. It must keep training/export actions unavailable until the required approval is established. A preparation PASS never means trained-model delivery, improved quality, production readiness or whole T-015 done. Human authorization and host/runtime evidence remain separate HOLD gates.

## Verdict boundaries

- **Preparation source PASS:** all applicable P1–P7 and Q1 obligations for the stated source unit pass; X/R implementations, if present, must satisfy their guard/refusal boundaries before such PASS. Name every unwired consumer and unfinished application obligation.
- **Human-gate HOLD:** exact corpus/export/training/promotion permissions absent; no real export/training is performed to evade this gate. Future approvals must be checked against the actual fingerprint and scope.
- **Host/live HOLD:** claimed real model/backend/OS/application consumer has not run successfully with retained evidence. A synthetic fixture proves only fixture behavior.
- **Full T-015 PASS:** applicable P/X/R/Q clauses pass with actual authorized export, training, downstream consumption and evaluation proof, plus independent checker verdict. If the implementation deliberately supplies only preparation, full T-015 remains open/HOLD. Central integration alone owns TASKS/.goal status updates; this contract does not close them.

Security class findings (tenant isolation, approvals, data writes and credentials) are never round-capped. Issue-fix verification replays the union-ledger issue's recorded reproductions verbatim and reports counts. No contract weakening to match a candidate; no mutation without timeout, byte-backup restoration on all exits and comparison proof.
