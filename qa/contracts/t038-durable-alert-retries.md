# Contract — t038-durable-alert-retries

Checker-owned source acceptance for the authorized durable lifecycle-alert retry seam. D-165 channels and the existing autojoin cycle-1 email ceiling remain authoritative. No live delivery, scheduler activation, or whole T-038/T-051 acceptance follows from this contract.

1. A failed enabled channel or a rate-limited unsent lifecycle channel retains bounded, sanitized replay data in the current tenant's alert-state file. A later tick drains it even when the lifecycle event no longer recurs, including when email is disabled.
2. Restart/reconstructed dependencies preserve the queue, successful per-channel dedupe, and the transport-attempt counter. A replay attempts only incomplete enabled channels; a successfully delivered dashboard/email/calendar channel is not repeated.
3. Transport failures have at most three attempts per session/kind/channel across original sends, drains and restarts. Rate-limited deferrals do not count as transport attempts. Exhausted work is removed or remains inert, with truthful bounded reporting.
4. Queue growth, lifetime and each drain are bounded; malformed/expired replay rows cannot execute. Overflow/drop/expiry is reported truthfully. Loading does not refresh the original queue age.
5. Tenant ownership is explicit and verified before replay. Foreign-tenant rows cannot send or enter another tenant's dashboard. Replay projects only accepted fields and types; URLs/control characters and unrecognized recipient/provider-command fields cannot enter stored payload or outbound data. The configured recipient is the only email destination; calendar remains private, transparent, no attendees, sendUpdates none and stored-id-only patching.
6. Existing email caps hold during replay: six ordinary, three individual failures and one digest per rolling hour with default configuration. Replay of the original ISS-AUTOJOIN-001 corpus is required: twelve about-to-join followed by twelve distinct-session capture-failed events, with every failure represented on dashboard/calendar.
7. The existing tick's flush consumer reaches durable retries and preserves its boolean digest-delivered result. Focused affected-stage, immediate downstream and contracts/verify_contracts.py evidence is required. No real GWS/email/Calendar/browser/provider call is permitted in these tests.

8. Root-authorized safety extension: a `rules-degraded` alert sends immediately once per verified tenant outage, including when the ordinary email cap is exhausted. This is a distinct safety email allowance in addition to C6's lifecycle ceiling. Failed delivery persists and obeys the same three-attempt bound. Repeated identical degradation callbacks during one outage do not repeat successful channels; a changed reason/snapshot may record a truthful dashboard transition, while the email remains once per outage. A healthy callback clears the outage identity so a later outage can alert again. Restart preserves the current outage identity. Rules-degraded has no synthetic meeting/calendar marker when start/end are absent; dashboard retains the truthful safety event. A full ordinary retry queue must not silently discard the first safety transport failure.

The rules producer and API/public-client/web consumer integration remain separately scoped: this alert-source contract alone cannot certify an immediate callback on an empty schedule tick or visible API/UI rendering of the added kind. The final verdict must name those remaining gates.
