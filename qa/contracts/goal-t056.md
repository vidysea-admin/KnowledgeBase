## Acceptance criteria

- **[C1] Real integrated reachability.** Running authenticated API and web application expose the completed Ask, policy and review experiences through normal navigation. Services use the configured real database, mounted handlers, supported issued scopes and authenticated actor identity; disconnected helpers, a simulator-only page, in-memory stores and synthetic page facades do not satisfy this criterion. Missing dependencies/configuration return honest errors.
- **[C2] Complete Ask input experience.** Evidence mode, effective source/scope filters, suggested questions and tenant-bound saved query/answer history work through the real API and UI. Filters actually constrain retrieval and final context; history survives reload/restart and cannot be read or overwritten by another tenant. Blank inputs issue no request; stale replies after an authentication change cannot expose an old tenant's answer/history.
- **[C3] Complete answer experience.** Answers show an honestly defined retrieval-confidence/coverage signal, supporting evidence excerpts, internal and web sources in separate labelled/countable lists, usable follow-ups and durable feedback. Confidence is never presented as factual certainty or a calibrated score without its required measurement. Insufficient coverage, unavailable fallback, policy hold/refusal and operational errors remain distinguishable. Follow-ups and feedback act on the correct answer/revision after reload and authentication changes.
- **[C4] Grounded provenance.** Internal answer evidence resolves in the same tenant to real source/session, turn, original excerpt, speaker label and timestamp; available retained recording evidence links or seeks to that moment. Missing media/provenance is explicit, never fabricated. Untrusted web schemes remain inert. Real sample answers are independently checked against their actual cited text, not merely the existence of source IDs; web evidence stays separately attributable. Claims withheld or rejected during ingestion/review cannot leak through lexical, vector, tree or generated-answer paths.
- **[C5] Durable authoring and history.** Users author validated bounded YAML policies, simulate a fresh authenticated Ask result, save sequential immutable revisions, inspect actor/time/change history and retire policies. YAML cannot execute code or accept arbitrary objects as tenant identity. Persistence survives process restart; stale-version races, invalid input, foreign policy IDs and gaps cannot replace another revision. Schemas/generated types/migrations and tenant-leading indexes comply with ARCHITECTURE rather than inventing an ungoverned collection shape.
- **[C6] Policies actually govern Ask.** Selecting an authenticated stored active policy binds the exact applied revision into the result/audit/history. Its mode/style/insufficient-coverage behavior governs real retrieval and generation: internal-only never invokes or incorporates web retrieval, requested style reaches the final-answer provider, and hold/refusal does not release an unapproved candidate as an accepted answer. Retired/missing/foreign/stale policy references fail safely. Simulation remains explicitly separate from saving/applying and cannot weaken mandatory citations.
- **[C7] Conflicting evidence resolution.** The product exposes actual contradictory source evidence relevant to an answer, with both original citations and a human-reviewable explanation. A reviewer can record a durable resolution, actor, reason and evidence/revision binding; affected answer behavior reflects that resolution without deleting source provenance or implying that acknowledgement establishes truth. Changed evidence cannot silently inherit an obsolete resolution. No specific automated semantic contradiction algorithm is mandated by the source catalogue: conservatively flagged or human-reported conflicts are acceptable when they exercise this complete evidence workflow. Policy-mode/style conflicts alone are insufficient.
- **[C8] AI answer review queue.** Actual candidate answers requiring review produce durable tenant-bound queue records containing the query, candidate, exact evidence and applied policy/source revisions. Authorized review can approve/reject with attributable reason; unauthorized/foreign/stale/duplicate reviews cannot publish or resolve another answer. Approval releases only the reviewed candidate/evidence version; rejection/refusal cannot be displayed as an approved answer. Queue/history and resolution survive restart and are usable in the web UI. A queue containing only policy changes/conflicts is insufficient.
- **[C9] Search acceptance.** The normal product search UI consumes real structured/lexical and unstructured/vector results side by side with source attribution, counts, effective filters and honest empty/error states. Approved WhatsApp/unstructured content is discoverable; rejected/sensitive/quarantined content is absent. A second tenant cannot retrieve rows, vectors, excerpts or citations from the first. This criterion accepts dependency evidence only when its actual mounted current-byte integration is demonstrated, not from file presence or a historical isolated PASS.
- **[C10] Dependency and semantic gates.** U3.1, U3.2, T-007, T-008 and T-022 complete their applicable acceptance or have an explicitly approved replacement, with referenced matching independent verdicts. T-022's thirty individual human reviews/live calibration cannot be replaced by agent-generated labels. Historical scoped PASSes and a catalogue scorer's page/collection probes do not close these whole-parent requirements.
- **[C11] Focused independent verification.** Current candidate manifest/packet supplies exact changed paths, cycle, hashes, caller/consumer map, commands, fixtures, outputs and durations. Checker directly reviews changed security boundaries and independently exercises authenticated tenancy, durable restart/race behavior, policy application, source-grounding and review-to-answer/search downstream consumption on nonproduction sample data. Applicable recorded issue reproductions are replayed verbatim with per-issue corpus counts. Required contracts/verify_contracts.py passes; an empty frozen-contract set is reported as vacuous. Tests obey CPU leases and mutation timeout/byte-restore safety.
- **[C12] Actual interactive and end-to-end proof.** Retained browser evidence from the real running app shows Ask input controls/history, real cited answer and source click-through, YAML save/reload/application, conflict and candidate-answer review and structured/vector search. Real database/process restart and authenticated HTTP receipts establish durability and boundaries. At least one real provider-backed sample answer is traced to actual source evidence. Browser/provider/production actions require their existing authorization; an unavailable prerequisite is HOLD, never simulated proof or a reason to downgrade this contract.
