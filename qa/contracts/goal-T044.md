## Acceptance criteria

1. **Actual pipeline wiring.** With `LKB_TRANSCRIPT_QA=1`, the transcription driver applies QA to each contributing span and the merged transcript before committing canonical turns. Span timing uses the actual span media duration; merged timing uses the independently probed recording duration, with offsets handled correctly. Rejected retries and incomplete attempts do not become final turns or silently contribute to the accepted QA verdict. D-162 still requires the flag OFF by default; disabled behavior retains its prior acceptance and failure boundaries.

2. **Timing and conservation.** For finite D > 0, every emitted turn has finite `0 <= tStart <= tEnd <= D`. Unknown, nonpositive, nonnumeric or nonfinite duration cannot emit unchecked turns. Invalid timing is reported and cannot silently obtain a clean verdict. Input objects remain unchanged; retained text, speaker fields and ordering are preserved. Every actual drop is indexed and reasoned, and changed/drop/count fields reconcile. Starts at or beyond D are dropped and flagged; ends exactly at D are preserved. The recorded 223-second-on-186-second reproduction reports the original 37-second excess and leaves no emitted timestamp beyond 186 seconds. Clamping repairs bounds; it does not prove semantic accuracy or remove the duty to report suspicious input.

3. **Honest hallucination spot-check.** Past-end starts, repeated normalized-text runs at the documented threshold, invalid timing and nonmonotonic ordering remain auditable. Signals from accepted spans survive into the final QA handoff even when clamping/dropping makes the merged transcript appear clean. Accepted attempts are explicitly distinguished from abandoned retries: a discarded bad attempt alone must not reject a later clean accepted generation. Repetition detection remains a conservative heuristic; no semantic hallucination guarantee or invented human adjudication is claimed. Real-audio spot-check evidence must state what was inspected and what remains uncertain.

4. **Generation-bound evidence.** The persisted QA artifact identifies the final canonical transcript bytes/generation, the authoritative audio duration, and the selected contributing span attempts. Consumers check the actual transcript digest/generation, not only its turn count. Missing required QA, stale same-count QA, wrong-duration QA, absent/malformed bindings, inconsistent counts/indices, or a report that omits accepted-span anomalies cannot certify a clean generation. Legacy unbound artifacts remain historical evidence and must not be silently upgraded or treated as fresh bound reports. Ordinary clamping alone is a repair; retained hallucination/invalidity signals require the applicable refusal/review path.

5. **Immediate downstream consumption.** The emitted turns and bound QA artifact pass through the real `validate-session` gate and `loadWebinarSession` consumer on an accepted clean sample. Suspicious or inconsistent samples refuse without a handwritten passed validation file or an unrelated partial-coverage override. Validation remains tied to the checked turns: later transcript changes invalidate downstream acceptance, including same-count changes. QA report publication failures or stale prior files must not leave an unchecked generation consumable as validated. Existing tenant/session identity, canonical-turn validation and source provenance protections remain effective.

6. **Real evidence and compatibility.** Before full T-044 PASS, provide attributable comparison on actual recorded audio and real provider-produced turns, with independently probed duration, input/output/report hashes, original and repaired timing/counts, retained-attempt evidence, and actual downstream result. Explain whether provider output was freshly generated or replayed from a proven recording-bound artifact; synthetic/fake transport tests alone cannot satisfy this criterion. Real provider calls, promotion, production writes and audio availability remain existing authorization/environment gates. If those prerequisites are absent, report partial implementation and HOLD whole-task completion rather than inventing output or weakening acceptance.

7. **Independent proof and handoff.** A `ready-for-check` maker manifest and packet specify the current fix cycle, all changed/new files and final hashes, exact commands/results and remaining gates. The checker reviews actual changed logic and immediate consumers, verifies final hashes before and after checks, and performs focused boundary probes when the root grants the CPU lease. Relevant issue fixes replay their ledger reproductions verbatim with counts by issue ID. Include `contracts/verify_contracts.py` evidence when applicable (explicitly note an empty/vacuous frozen-contract set), affected-stage sample evidence and immediate downstream evidence. Only a matching independent full-scope PASS permits task closure; scoped PASS, HOLD or missing live evidence leaves T-044 in progress.
