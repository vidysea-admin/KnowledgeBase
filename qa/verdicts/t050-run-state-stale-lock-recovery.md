DANGEROUS DIRECTION (takeover against a live writer):
1a Stall after the token re-check. Stall 31 s: B `locked`. 59 min: B `locked`. 61 min: B takes over, saves, and REAPS A's temp file, so A's rename gets ENOENT: A=io-error, B's state stands, nothing lost. Stall 2 h before the re-check: A=`locked`, nothing lost. Clock stepped +2 h at every operation of A's save (19 positions): one position (A inside its own token re-check) lets A reach the rename; again stopped by the reap (A=io-error). So: displacement of a live local owner needs a stall >= 1 h; the data rename itself does NOT re-validate `expected`; the only thing stopping a post-re-check stall is the reap. With B's reap unlink made to fail (EPERM on *.tmp): A=ok, A's state overwrites B's, B's acknowledged save is lost (reproduced). Reachable only with a >= 1 h stall inside the one-syscall gap AND a failed best-effort unlink: outside the stated envelope and not an interleaving of ordinary load. Ruled a documented residual (contract P-R1), ISS-RUNLOCK-003 medium, BEFORE-WIRING. Not FAIL: the lease bound is the standard limit of a rename-based lock, nothing is reachable below 1 h, and the double condition is explicit. A reader who weighs it as FAIL-level should read it as the one judgement call in this verdict.
1b Empty-body window: the lock is `wx`-created and its body written in a second step (not temp+link), so an empty lock exists. Real NTFS, injected 60 ms pause at the creator's body write, contender with staleMs 20 ms: B=`locked` (its claim rename onto the creator's open handle is refused), A wrote its body and saved ok. On POSIX the creator's later save would be stopped by the token re-check. A crash there leaves an empty lock recoverable after staleMs (ISS-RUNLOCK-001, low, bounded). Not reachable as two holders on this host.
1c The 1 h ceiling displaces a live owner by design (pid-reuse bound); there is no heartbeat. Safe for data per 1a (token re-check + reap); a save lasts milliseconds, so a heartbeat is unnecessary. Ruling: the ceiling is acceptable; ISS-RUNLOCK-003 is what to fix.
