## Ruling-1 logic: verified independently

- **Source:** `auto-join.ts:228-235`. Inside everyWebinar, any Gmail candidate that is neither `preTrusted` nor `senderVerified` is skipped:
  - `preTrusted` is `status==="approved"`, or `status==="auto_approved"` with `senderAuthenticated===true`;
  - `senderVerified` is `senderAuthenticated===true`.
- **Probe:** `probe.out` (exit 0) replays each case against both the base copy and the candidate.
- **Results, everyWebinar mode:**
  - Missing status: `false` gives untrusted-sender, undefined gives needs-review, `true` schedules.
  - Garbage status (`"garbage"`, `"APPROVED"`): same as missing status.
  - `senderAuthenticated` of `"true"`, `1`, `"false"` or `null`: needs-review, so not scheduled.
  - `auto_approved` with `false`, undefined or `"true"`: not scheduled. `auto_approved` with `true`: schedules.
  - `approved` with any auth value: schedules.
  - Rejected: refused.
  - unsafe=0: no candidate was scheduled without either human approval or `senderAuthenticated===true`.
- **everyWebinar=false:** 40 inputs (empty allowlist and evil.com allowlisted), plus the calendar-only case. Results are identical to base (diffs=0).
- **Loader probe:** `loader-probe.out` (exit 0). `true` maps to `true` and `false` to `false`. `"true"`, `1`, `"yes"`, `null`, absent, `"false"` and `0` all map to `undefined`. Only the row with `true` comes out authenticated.
- **Loader consumers:** I checked `calendar-client.ts:53/67/86`, `registration-workflow.ts:38/112`, `join-rules-approved-trust.ts:50` and `zoom-registration-proof.ts:130`. All use `=== true`, `!== true`, falsy checks or a typeof guard, so mapping unknown to `undefined` instead of `false` changes nothing for them.
