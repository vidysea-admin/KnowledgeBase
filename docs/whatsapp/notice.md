# WhatsApp archiving notice

Read this before you ask to link your WhatsApp number.

- Messages from the WhatsApp groups you choose to track will be archived in the knowledge base. They are visible to you and to the knowledge-base administrators.
- Nothing is archived from a group you have not marked.
- The archiving client is unofficial. WhatsApp can ban a number that uses it, and that risk is yours.
- You can unlink at any time. Unlinking stops new archiving; knowledge that was already approved from your messages stays in the knowledge base.
